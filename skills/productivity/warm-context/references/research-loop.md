# Research loop (one subtopic)

Input: the pack's Scope (goal, constraints, out of scope), one subtopic, and the sources the user allowed.

1. **Search wide.** Run 3–6 searches that phrase the subtopic in different ways. Include the terms practitioners actually use.
2. **Read primary sources first:** official docs, specs, standards bodies, established reference works, and practitioners with first-hand experience. Treat SEO listicles and AI-generated content farms as leads, not evidence.
3. **Extract knowledge, not procedure:** concepts, vocabulary, the options and what each trades off, rules of thumb with the reason behind them, failure modes and their causes. Write a source URL against every claim.
4. **Log the edges.** Anything relevant that you came across but didn't research goes into a *Seen but not researched* list. This is how the pack shows its own limits.
5. **Flag disagreement.** Where sources conflict, record both positions and who holds each one. Don't pick a winner silently.

Done when the subtopic's findings would let a planner reason about the choices in it, every claim has a source, and the edges list is filled in. If the findings are thin after two rounds of searching, stop and say so.

Return: findings (with sources), the edges list, and any conflicts.
