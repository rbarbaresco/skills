# How to restart the office router

Restart the router when the office Wi-Fi is up but nothing loads. It takes about five minutes. You don't need admin access.

1. Find the router: the black box on the shelf above the printer, labelled "NET-01".
2. Unplug its power cable (the round one, not the yellow network cables).
3. Wait 30 seconds.
4. Plug the power cable back in.
5. Wait until the light labelled "Internet" turns solid green. This takes two to three minutes.

If the Internet light is still orange after five minutes, don't restart it again. Post in #it-help with a photo of the lights.

Don't press the small reset button on the back. It wipes the configuration, and only IT can restore it.
