# Introducing Notely 4.2: A New Era of Seamless Productivity

In today's fast-paced digital landscape, staying organized isn't just a nice-to-have — it's essential. That's why we're thrilled to unveil Notely 4.2, a pivotal update that underscores our unwavering commitment to empowering users everywhere.

## Overview

This release represents a testament to our team's dedication. It's not just an update; it's a reimagining of how you capture, organize, and share your ideas. Let's dive in!

## Key Features

- **Offline Mode:** Offline mode is here! You can now create and edit notes without a connection on iOS and Android, with changes syncing seamlessly when you're back online.
- **Smarter Sync:** Sync has been dramatically enhanced. Sync conflicts dropped from 3.1% of syncs to 0.4% in our beta, ensuring a robust and frictionless experience.
- **CSV Export:** Export your tables to CSV, unlocking powerful new workflows and fostering collaboration across your ecosystem.

## Pricing

We're committed to delivering value. Notely Pro remains $8 per month — no price changes, no surprises.

## What's Next

While we're incredibly excited about this release, we also want to be transparent: the Windows desktop app, originally planned for this release, has moved to Q3. We believe this will allow us to deliver an even more polished experience.

## Conclusion

Notely 4.2 is available today, March 12, 2026. In summary, with offline mode, smarter sync, and CSV export, Notely 4.2 marks a significant milestone in our journey. The future of productivity is bright — and we can't wait for you to be part of it. Happy noting! 🚀
